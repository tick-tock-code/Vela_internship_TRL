Evaluate each of the following dimensions on a scale from 1 to 5, focusing exclusively on the founder's profile and supporting evidence. Use clear, unbiased language for each dimension, ensuring their intent is preserved:

- Demonstrated growth potential and momentum
- Depth of ownership and sense of responsibility
- Alignment and consistency in career path and focus
- Creativity and resourcefulness in overcoming challenges

Then determine an overall founder quality score based on the following criteria:

- Top-tier founder:
- Exhibits rapid development or leadership early on
- Shows strong ownership in environments with ambiguity
- Operates within high-growth sectors or technical domains

- Lower-tier founder:
- Demonstrates limited ownership or responsibility
- Progresses slowly or inconsistently
- Lacks clear direction or focus

In cases where evidence is mixed, assign a middle score and prioritize the strongest supporting evidence in the profile. Emphasize consistency and concrete evidence throughout your evaluation.
