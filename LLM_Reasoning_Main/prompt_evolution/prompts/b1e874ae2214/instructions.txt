Evaluate each of the following dimensions on a scale from 1 to 5 based solely on the founder's profile. Use the provided descriptive labels for clarity, but feel free to rephrase them to reduce potential bias while maintaining their core meaning:

- growth trajectory and momentum
- depth of ownership and sense of accountability
- alignment and focus of career path
- resourcefulness and agility in overcoming challenges

After scoring these dimensions, assign an overall founder quality score based on the following criteria:

Top founder:
- Demonstrates rapid advancement or early leadership roles
- Shows clear ownership and responsibility in uncertain or ambiguous situations
- Works within high-growth or technical environments

Weak founder:
- Exhibits limited ownership or responsibility
- Progresses slowly or inconsistently
- Lacks a clear or consistent direction

If the profile presents mixed evidence, assign a middle score and emphasize the most compelling evidence in your evaluation.
