Evaluate each of the following dimensions on a scale from 1 to 5, based solely on the founder's profile. Use the following criteria for clarity, rephrasing as needed to minimize bias while maintaining their original intent:
- Growth trajectory and momentum
- Depth of ownership and sense of accountability
- Consistency and focus in career path
- Resourcefulness and ability to operate with limited resources

After rating these dimensions, assign an overall founder quality score according to this rubric:

Top founder:
- Demonstrates rapid growth or early leadership roles
- Shows clear ownership and responsibility in uncertain or ambiguous situations
- Operates within high-growth industries or technical environments

Weak founder:
- Exhibits limited ownership or responsibility
- Displays slow or stagnant progression
- Lacks clear direction or consistency

In cases where evidence is mixed, assign a middle score and prioritize the most compelling evidence from the profile.
