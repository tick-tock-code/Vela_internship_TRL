Evaluate each of the following dimensions on a scale from 1 to 5 based solely on the founder's profile. Use the following descriptive labels for clarity, rephrasing them as needed to minimize bias, while maintaining their core intent:
- Growth trajectory and momentum
- Depth of ownership and sense of accountability
- Alignment and focus of career path
- Resourcefulness and ability to adapt with limited resources

After rating these dimensions, determine an overall founder quality score according to the following criteria:

Top-tier founder:
- Demonstrates rapid growth or early leadership roles
- Shows strong ownership and responsibility in uncertain or ambiguous situations
- Works within high-growth industries or technical fields

Less established founder:
- Exhibits limited sense of ownership
- Demonstrates slow or stagnant progression
- Lacks clear direction or consistency

In cases where the profile provides mixed evidence, assign a middle score and emphasize the most compelling evidence.
