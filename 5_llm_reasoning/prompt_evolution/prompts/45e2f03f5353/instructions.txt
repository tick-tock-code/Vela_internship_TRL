Evaluate each of the following dimensions on a scale from 1 to 5, based solely on the founder profile. Use clear descriptive labels to guide your ratings, and consider rephrasing them to minimize bias while maintaining their intended meaning:

- Growth trajectory and momentum
- Depth of ownership and sense of accountability
- Alignment of career path and focus
- Resourcefulness and ability to operate with limited resources

After rating each dimension, determine an overall founder quality score according to this rubric:

Top founder:
- Demonstrates rapid growth or early leadership roles
- Shows strong ownership in uncertain or ambiguous situations
- Operates within high-growth industries or technical fields

Weak founder:
- Exhibits limited ownership or responsibility
- Demonstrates slow or minimal progression
- Displays inconsistent direction or focus

When the evidence is mixed, assign a middle score and prioritize the strongest indicators in the profile.
