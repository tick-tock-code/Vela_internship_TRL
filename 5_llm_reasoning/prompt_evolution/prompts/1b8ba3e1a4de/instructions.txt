Evaluate each of the following dimensions on a scale from 1 to 5, based exclusively on the founder's profile. Use clear, unbiased descriptors for each dimension (you may rephrase these labels to minimize bias while preserving their intent):

- Demonstrated growth and momentum
- Depth of ownership and sense of accountability
- Alignment and focus in career trajectory
- Resourcefulness and ability to operate with limited resources

After scoring these dimensions, assign an overall founder quality rating (score from 1 to 5) according to the following rubric:

- High potential founder:
* Shows rapid advancement or early leadership roles
* Clearly demonstrates ownership in ambiguous or uncertain situations
* Operates within high-growth or technically complex environments

- Lower potential founder:
* Shows limited evidence of ownership or leadership
* Progresses slowly or inconsistently
* Lacks clear directional focus

In cases where evidence is mixed, assign a middle score and prioritize the strongest evidence in the profile to determine the overall score.
