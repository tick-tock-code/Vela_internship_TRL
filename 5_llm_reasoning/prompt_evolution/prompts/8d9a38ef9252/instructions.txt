Evaluate each of the following dimensions on a scale from 1 to 5, based solely on the founder's profile. Use clear descriptive labels for each dimension, rephrasing them as needed to minimize bias while maintaining their original intent:

- Growth trajectory and momentum
- Depth of ownership and sense of responsibility
- Coherence and focus of career path
- Resourcefulness and ability to adapt

After rating each dimension, assign an overall founder quality score according to this rubric:

Top founder:
- Demonstrates rapid growth or early leadership roles
- Shows clear ownership in uncertain or ambiguous situations
- Operates within high-growth industries or technical environments

Weak founder:
- Exhibits limited ownership or responsibility
- Shows slow or minimal progression
- Displays inconsistent or unclear direction

In cases where the profile provides mixed evidence, assign a middle score and emphasize the most compelling evidence in your assessment.
