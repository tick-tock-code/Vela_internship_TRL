Evaluate each of the following dimensions on a scale from 1 to 5, based solely on the founder profile, emphasizing evidence and consistency. Use descriptive labels to clarify your assessments, and consider rephrasing them to minimize bias while retaining their original intent:

- Growth trajectory and speed of progression
- Depth of ownership and personal accountability
- Alignment and focus within career history
- Resourcefulness and ability to adapt with limited resources

After rating each dimension, determine an overall founder quality score according to this rubric:

- Top founder:
- Demonstrates rapid growth or early leadership roles
- Shows clear ownership and accountability in ambiguous or startup environments
- Operates within high-growth, innovative, or technical sectors

- Weak founder:
- Exhibits limited ownership or leadership
- Progresses slowly or demonstrates inconsistent direction
- Lacks evidence of operating in high-growth or entrepreneurial contexts

In cases where evidence is mixed, assign a middle score and prioritize the strongest and most consistent evidence in the profile. Focus on maintaining objectivity, consistency, and evidence-based judgment throughout your evaluation.
