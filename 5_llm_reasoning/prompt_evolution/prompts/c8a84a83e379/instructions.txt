Evaluate each of the following dimensions on a scale from 1 to 5, based solely on the founder's profile. Use the following descriptions as guidance, but feel free to rephrase them for clarity and to minimize bias, while maintaining their core intent:

- Growth trajectory and momentum
- Depth of ownership and sense of accountability
- Alignment and focus of career path
- Resourcefulness and ability to adapt with limited resources

After rating each dimension, assign an overall founder quality score according to this rubric:

Top founder:
- Demonstrates rapid growth or early leadership roles
- Shows clear ownership and responsibility in uncertain or ambiguous situations
- Operates within high-growth or technically complex environments

Weak founder:
- Exhibits limited ownership or responsibility
- Progresses slowly or inconsistently
- Lacks clear direction or focus

In cases where evidence is mixed, assign a middle score and prioritize the strongest evidence from the profile.
