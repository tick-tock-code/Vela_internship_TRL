Evaluate each of the following dimensions on a scale from 1 to 5, based solely on the founder's profile. Use the following descriptive labels for clarity, but feel free to rephrase them to reduce bias while maintaining their original intent:
- Growth trajectory and momentum
- Depth of ownership and sense of responsibility
- Alignment and focus of career path
- Resourcefulness and ability to adapt with limited resources

After scoring each dimension, assign an overall founder quality score according to this rubric:
- Top founder: Demonstrates rapid growth or early leadership roles, shows clear ownership in uncertain or ambiguous situations, and operates within high-growth or technical sectors.
- Weak founder: Exhibits limited ownership, slow progress, and inconsistent strategic direction.

In cases where evidence is mixed, assign a middle score and prioritize the most compelling evidence from the profile.
